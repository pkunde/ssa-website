Bookkeeping services

Service frequency and scope can be discussed based on the size of your business, transaction volume and accounting system.

Day-to-day accounting

Recording and classification of business transactions using the information and supporting documents provided.

Ledger maintenance

Organising ledger accounts and reviewing entries for completeness and consistency within the agreed scope.

Bank reconciliation

Comparing bank statements with book balances and listing items that require clarification or adjustment.

Financial statements

Preparation support for periodic income and expense summaries, balance sheet information and related schedules.

Receivables & payables

Organising customer and supplier balances and helping track outstanding items based on available records.

Payroll records

Assistance compiling salary inputs, employee payment records and related deduction information.

Records to maintain

Good records make compliance easier

Depending on the assignment, useful records may include:

  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • Bank statements and cash records
  • Expense bills and payment proofs
  • Customer and supplier ledgers
  • Loan, asset and liability details
  • Payroll and employee payment records
  • Previous period trial balance and financial statements

Flexible support for your requirements

Whether you need help organising monthly transactions or preparing information for a particular reporting period, we can discuss the volume of work and the records available.

Accounting records are prepared based on the information and documents supplied. Management remains responsible for the completeness and accuracy of underlying business records.
