Documents checklist

Keep your income and tax records together

  • PAN and Aadhaar details, as applicable
  • Form 16 / salary statements, if salaried
  • Bank statements and interest certificates
  • Form 26AS, AIS and TIS information
  • Investment, deduction and insurance proofs, where relevant
  • Capital gains statements or property transaction documents, if applicable
  • Business or professional income and expense records, if applicable
  • Previous year's return and tax payment details

Why accurate information matters

Income from different sources, tax credits, deductions and disclosures should be reviewed together. Please share complete information, including income or transactions that may not appear in a salary statement.

Tax rules, forms and deadlines may change. The correct filing position depends on your individual facts and the law applicable to the relevant assessment year.

Income tax FAQs

Can you file returns for salaried individuals?

We can assist with return preparation for salaried taxpayers. The documents needed depend on other income sources, deductions and transactions, if any.

Do I need to share Form 26AS and AIS?

These records can help review tax credits and reported financial information. Keep them available along with your other income documents.

Can you help if I have received a notice?

Yes, you can contact us with the notice and relevant return details. We can discuss the nature of the notice and the assistance required.

Planning to file your income tax return?

Contact us with the assessment year and your income category.
